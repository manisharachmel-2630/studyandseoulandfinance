My Korean Learning Journey

📅 October 9, 2026 • 5 min read

🌸 Why I Am Learning Korean

I am learning Korean because I love Korean culture, music and traditions. I hope to visit South Korea someday and experience its beautiful places.

📚 How I Practise

I practise writing Hangul, learning new words, watching Korean videos and listening to Korean songs. I try to study every day and improve little by little.

  • Practise Korean letters and pronunciation.
  • Learn useful everyday sentences.
  • Listen to Korean conversations.
  • Revise vocabulary regularly.

🎯 My Goal

My goal is to speak Korean confidently and visit Seoul in the future. I know that learning a language takes time, so I will keep practising.

🌷 Final Thoughts

Every new word is a small step towards my dream. If you are learning something new too, keep going!
