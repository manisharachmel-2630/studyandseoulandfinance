🇰🇷 How I Started Learning Korean as a Beginner

Beginner Guide • 2026

Learning a new language can feel difficult at first, but starting with small daily lessons makes it easier.

1. Learn Hangul first

Start with Korean consonants and vowels and practice combining them into syllables.

2. Learn useful everyday words

Focus on greetings, numbers, food, places and simple expressions that you can use regularly.

3. Practice every day

Even 20–30 minutes of reading, listening and sentence practice can help you build a routine.

4. Make learning enjoyable

Try Korean songs, videos and simple conversations while continuing structured study.

Conclusion

You don't need to learn everything at once. Start small, practice consistently and track your progress.
